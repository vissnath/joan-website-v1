The approach

The Playbook Engine

Joan extracts and analyzes the work your team has already done. No need to build a structure from scratch: Joan retrieves the decisions from contracts you've already negotiated. One pass produces three things.

Output 01

A draft playbook

Built from the positions your team actually takes. Taken from the redlines, not invented.

Output 02

A clear filing structure

Every document is tagged by counterparty, deal type, clause and version, so everything is easy to find.

Output 03

A searchable decision history

Everything that was decided, ready the moment a similar question comes up again.

The Contract Reviewer

With your playbook as the framework, Joan reviews every contract quickly and consistently.

Output 01

Reviewed against your playbook

Add a contract and Joan reviews it against your extracted and signed-off playbook.

Output 02

Risks flagged, redlines suggested

Joan flags risks based on your pre-approved positions and suggests redlines and comments.

Output 03

Version control during negotiation

Every round is tracked, so you always know what changed and what was agreed.

Small add-on, with big impact

The honest division of labor

Your contracts show what your team decided, not why. Joan drafts the playbook within a day. Your team adds the judgment where it matters.

Your data

In short: your data stays yours, is stored securely and separately, is not used to train the AI, and we arrange everything properly with a data processing agreement.

What data does Joan use?

Contracts, previous negotiation outcomes and the agreements in your playbook. Joan from Legal has no access to any systems other than the ones you open up for it.

Where is the data stored?

Your data is stored in [country/region, e.g. the EU] with [hosting provider]. It stays within a secure environment that is yours alone.

Who has access?

Only the people you designate. Neither Joan nor our team looks at the content, unless you ask us to for support.

Is the AI trained on our data?

No. Your data is only used to create your playbook and your reviews. It is not used to train the AI and never ends up with other customers. [To check: which AI model does Joan use, and what do its terms say about training and retention?]
